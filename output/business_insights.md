# Car Sales Business Insights

## 1. Executive Summary

This analysis examines car sales performance across manufacturers, vehicle models, pricing, fuel efficiency, and vehicle characteristics.

The dataset contains 117 cleaned records and 16 columns. The data quality analysis confirmed that the final dataset contains no duplicate rows and no missing values.

The analysis reveals that Ford is the strongest-performing manufacturer in terms of total sales, while the Ford F-Series is the highest-selling vehicle in the dataset.

---

## 2. Top-Selling Vehicles

The Ford F-Series is the best-selling vehicle in the dataset, with approximately 540.56 thousand units sold.

Other high-performing vehicles include:

* Ford Explorer
* Toyota Camry
* Ford Taurus
* Honda Accord
* Dodge Ram Pickup
* Ford Ranger
* Honda Civic

### Key Insight

Pickup trucks and popular family vehicles demonstrate strong sales performance. The Ford F-Series significantly outperforms other individual models in the dataset.

---

## 3. Manufacturer Performance

Ford is the strongest manufacturer based on total sales performance.

Ford achieved approximately 1,846.96 thousand total sales, significantly ahead of other manufacturers.

The next strongest manufacturers include:

* Dodge
* Toyota
* Honda
* Chevrolet

### Key Insight

Ford has a strong market position due to the combined performance of multiple successful models, including the F-Series, Explorer, Taurus, and Ranger.

---

## 4. Price and Sales by Vehicle Type

The analysis shows differences between vehicle categories in terms of average price and average sales.

Passenger vehicles have a higher average price, while the vehicle category labelled "Car" demonstrates higher average sales.

### Key Insight

Higher pricing does not necessarily result in higher sales. Vehicle affordability and market demand appear to have an important influence on sales performance.

---

## 5. Fuel Efficiency Analysis

Saturn has the highest average fuel efficiency among the manufacturers in the dataset.

Other manufacturers with strong fuel efficiency include:

* Chevrolet
* Hyundai
* Plymouth
* Volkswagen
* Toyota

### Key Insight

Manufacturers with efficient vehicles may appeal to customers who prioritize lower operating costs and fuel economy.

---

## 6. Value-for-Money Analysis

The value-for-money analysis uses horsepower relative to vehicle price as a performance indicator.

The Toyota Tacoma ranks highest using the horsepower-to-price metric.

Other strong value-for-money vehicles include:

* Hyundai Elantra
* Dodge Ram Pickup
* Dodge Neon
* Chevrolet Malibu

### Key Insight

Affordable vehicles with relatively strong engine performance can provide attractive value to customers.

---

## 7. Sales-to-Price Performance

The Ford F-Series has the strongest sales-to-price ratio in the dataset.

Other strong performers include:

* Ford Ranger
* Honda Civic
* Honda Accord
* Toyota Camry

### Key Insight

Several relatively affordable and popular vehicles achieve strong market performance, suggesting that price competitiveness can be an important factor in sales success.

---

## 8. Horsepower and Sales

The horsepower analysis shows that vehicles with the highest horsepower are not necessarily the highest-selling vehicles.

For example, some high-performance and luxury vehicles have strong horsepower ratings but relatively low sales.

### Key Insight

Horsepower alone is not a reliable predictor of sales performance. Factors such as price, practicality, brand popularity, and customer demand may have a stronger influence.

---

## 9. Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

### Focus on High-Demand Vehicle Categories

Manufacturers should continue investing in practical and high-demand vehicles, including popular trucks and family-oriented vehicles.

### Maintain Competitive Pricing

The analysis suggests that vehicles with competitive pricing can achieve strong sales performance.

### Improve Fuel Efficiency

Fuel efficiency can provide an important competitive advantage, particularly for customers focused on operating costs.

### Balance Performance and Affordability

Vehicles offering strong performance at a reasonable price may provide greater value and attract more customers.

### Use Multiple Factors for Sales Strategy

Sales performance should not be evaluated using horsepower alone. Pricing, vehicle type, fuel efficiency, practicality, and brand recognition should all be considered.

---

## 10. Conclusion

The Car Sales Analysis demonstrates how data cleaning, SQL analysis, and visualization can be combined to generate meaningful business insights.

The results show that Ford is the leading manufacturer in the dataset, the Ford F-Series is the strongest-selling vehicle, and factors such as affordability, practicality, fuel efficiency, and brand strength all contribute to sales performance.

This analysis provides a foundation for future improvements, including interactive dashboards and machine learning models for sales prediction.
